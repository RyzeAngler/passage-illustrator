# Visual review and revision

Generate and open each candidate individually. Start with the full image before editorial captions have been added. Independently describe what the image shows, then compare those observations with the passage and storyboard. Enlarge decisive details and finally compare related illustrations side by side. A contact sheet, generation summary, or successful file check cannot supply the evidence visible in the artwork itself.

## Three areas to inspect

| Area | Evidence to consult | What to inspect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fidelity to the source | Passage, point in the story, and documented artistic choices. | Identities, species, counts, actions and their targets, chronology, abilities, possession, and outcome. Does the illustration convey what happens here? |
| Physical and spatial coherence | Anatomy, object construction, action, viewpoint, and surroundings. | Limb articulation, left and right, grip, support, balance, contact with the ground, intersecting forms, occlusion, axes, joints, scale, and perspective. |
| Continuity | Established designs, relevant earlier images, and adjacent scenes. | Faces, build, clothing at this stage, material and proportions of props, fixed spatial relationships, and current state. Can the story, action, or viewpoint account for each change? |

Assess transformations and extraordinary abilities according to the work's own premises. The shape, space occupied, and points of contact must still make sense within the scene. Continuity allows motivated changes in pose, expression, time, and viewing angle.

## Decide whether to accept an image

- Define the critical requirements in the storyboard. For each applicable check, record the requirement, the observation, its basis, and the finding. Use **Pass**, **Error**, or **Unclear**. If a check is inapplicable, state why.
- When evidence is unclear, enlarge the area or inspect the original artwork. If framing, occlusion, or composition makes the detail unreadable, revise the image. A tentative guess cannot establish a pass.
- Correct every confirmed error. Accept a candidate as master artwork only after all applicable critical checks pass and known errors have been resolved. Withhold uncertain or incorrect features from the references used for later images.
- Give high-risk details a second independent look. These include the first use of a shared design, interacting or grappling figures, an object passing between several hands, exact counts, and changes of form. Judge again from the artwork. Use a second reviewer when available.
- Retain the master at its original pixel dimensions, with props and lettering within the scene resolved and editorial captions still separate. Fit it to the delivery dimensions once at export.

## Revise at the source of the problem

| Finding | Correction and extent of review |
| --- | --- |
| The prompt is ambiguous, contradictory, or omits a critical relationship. | Reconcile the storyboard and the complete prompt. Redraw or edit the affected illustration. |
| A face, hand, foot, piece of headwear, or prop detail is wrong. | Edit the defect and any connected structures. Check the whole figure or object, its contact points, and the overall composition. |
| The event, viewpoint, composition, or spatial arrangement is wrong. | Return to the storyboard and redesign the scene. Covering the error or applying a local patch will leave the underlying arrangement unresolved. |
| The same incorrect feature appears in several images. | Check the shared design and the references actually supplied. Reinspect every affected image after correcting the source. |
| Fixed spatial relationships drift between images drawn from the same viewpoint. | Limit edits to the necessary area of the accepted artwork. Where pixels must remain identical, preserve those areas by compositing and verify them. |
| Lettering on an object is incorrect. | Correct the inscription character by character. Recheck its surface, perspective, material, and position. |
| An editorial caption, type size, or placement is wrong. | Change the typesetting configuration and export the artwork again. |

After each correction, inspect the entire image, the edited area, and related images. An unchanged region still needs an existing valid review before its earlier finding can be carried forward.

Changing an image, viewpoint, or reference invalidates the findings it affects. Carry a finding forward only when both the content and its relevant relationships remain intact. Recheck doubtful cases. The current review must cover all applicable requirements, including those outside the last edit.

## Record the current review

For each illustration and cover, record its ID, the path and hash of the master or finished image, the reviewer, the passage and reference versions checked, observations and findings by item, open issues, production-review status, and user-review status.

Associate the master artwork and the finished export with their respective hashes. Revisit the relevant findings when a reference changes. Maintain a current record. Make temporary detail views or comparison sheets when they help resolve a question. There is no need to retain a separate report or screenshot for every revision.
